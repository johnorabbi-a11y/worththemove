Sources and current figures

Official routes for purchase tax and background information on mortgage comparisons.

What is verified, and what is illustrative

Rates, property values, fees and growth inputs are editable illustrations, not current market averages. Purchase tax defaults to zero as an unfilled allowance. Obtain the correct tax amount for the nation, ownership history, residency and transaction before relying on a purchase comparison.

  • MoneyHelper: remortgaging costs and comparisons

    Fees, switching costs, lender eligibility and loan-to-value. Rates in our examples are illustrative, not quoted from this source.

    Source page checked 2026-09-28
  • MoneyHelper: paying off a mortgage early

    Overpayment charges, access to cash and comparison with savings. No universal allowance is assumed.

    Source page checked 2026-09-28
  • GOV.UK: Stamp Duty Land Tax

    England and Northern Ireland purchase tax. Use the linked official calculator and enter the result.

    Source page checked 2026-09-28
  • Welsh Government: Land Transaction Tax calculator

    Wales purchase tax. No tax bands are stored in this website.

    Source page checked 2026-09-28
  • Revenue Scotland: Land and Buildings Transaction Tax calculator

    Scotland purchase tax. Use the official calculator and confirm the applicable amount with your conveyancer.

    Source page checked 2026-09-28

Information to obtain yourself

Use current lender illustrations for rates, fees and repayment charges; conveyancer quotes for legal costs and tax treatment; survey and removal quotes for transaction costs; and a scoped professional estimate for building works. Our example figures are not substitutes for those quotes.

Calculation formulas and limitations are maintained separately from changeable product or tax information.
