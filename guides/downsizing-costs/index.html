What costs are involved in downsizing?

A cheaper replacement home may release equity, but the amount available is lower than the headline price gap.

Pay off the debt first

Sale proceeds first cover the mortgage. The replacement price and transaction costs come next. A £300,000 sale with a £50,000 mortgage and a £220,000 purchase releases £30,000 before fees, not £80,000.

Compare recurring costs

Energy, council tax, service charges, insurance and maintenance might change. A smaller leasehold property can have substantial service charges, so size alone is not a reliable cost proxy. Enter matching categories for both homes.

Separate release from savings

Released equity was already yours. Moving converts it into accessible cash; it does not create new wealth. The calculator compares future equity and savings after transaction costs using equal monthly budgets. It does not assess benefits eligibility, inheritance planning or retirement suitability.
