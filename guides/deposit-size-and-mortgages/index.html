How deposit size affects a mortgage

A larger deposit reduces borrowing. A rate reduction may provide a second benefit, but it needs a real quote.

Two effects to separate

On a £200,000 home, increasing the deposit from 5% to 10% reduces borrowing by £10,000. Even at the same rate that lowers interest. If a lender offers a lower rate at the new LTV, compare that separately from the smaller balance.

The cash has an alternative use

Money put into a deposit cannot simultaneously earn savings interest or fund repairs. The calculator keeps the deposit difference in savings for the smaller-deposit option. It does not model waiting to accumulate the extra deposit.

A valuation can move the boundary

The lender’s valuation may differ from the agreed price. A lower valuation can push the borrowing into another LTV band or require more cash. Check the product’s actual limit and leave room for purchase costs.
