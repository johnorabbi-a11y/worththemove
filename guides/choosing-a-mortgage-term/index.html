Should you choose a shorter mortgage term?

A shorter term increases the required payment and usually reduces interest under the same rate.

Obligation versus flexibility

The shorter term commits you to a higher payment every month. A longer term with voluntary overpayments can produce a similar repayment path, but only if the lender allows the payments and you actually make them.

Use two time horizons

Compare the balance after a common five-year period, then examine full-term interest. The lifetime figure assumes an unchanged rate, making it useful for understanding the structure rather than forecasting decades of actual payments.

Stress the payment

Test a higher rate and consider how the required payment would fit after a change in income. The tool does not conduct affordability checks or model retirement income. Confirm the term available to you with a lender.
