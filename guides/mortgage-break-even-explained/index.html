What mortgage break-even means

Break-even is a crossing between two cost paths. Define the costs before relying on the date.

Cash-flow and economic break-even differ

Dividing a fee by the monthly payment reduction estimates a cash-flow payback. That can be misleading when one mortgage repays principal faster. Our deal tools compare accumulated interest and fees each month, with principal and balances shown separately.

Read a crossing carefully

If Option B is first cheaper in month 19, it has recovered its initial cost under the entered rates by that date. Leaving in month 12 would tell a different story. A later reset can reverse the advantage; a crossing is not a promise of permanent savings.

When there is no crossing

There may be no break-even within the chosen period, or the fee-paying option may always cost more. Extend the period only when the same rate assumptions still make sense. Include an exit charge if the decision would trigger one.
