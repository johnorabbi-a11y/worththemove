How product fees change the real mortgage cost

A cheaper rate can come with a larger fixed cost. The balance and the time you keep the deal determine whether it pays back.

Start with the whole bill

List the product fee, valuation, legal costs, exit charges and any cashback. Keep the mortgage balance, remaining term and comparison period consistent. A fee buys access to a particular product; it does not pay down your original mortgage.

Paying now or borrowing the fee

Paying a £999 fee in cash reduces savings immediately. Adding it to the mortgage increases borrowing and interest. Our comparison counts the fee in both cases, but only the financed version attracts mortgage interest. The lender may also consider the added fee when checking its lending limits.

Find the useful threshold

For a small balance, even a noticeable rate reduction may save too little interest to cover a fixed fee. Try the balance threshold and compare periods that end before a likely move or next deal change. A threshold based on unchanged rates stops being useful when the assumptions change.
