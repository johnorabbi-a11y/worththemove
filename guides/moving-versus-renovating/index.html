How to compare moving with improving your home

Both routes can buy a better living arrangement, but the financial paths and practical trade-offs differ.

Make the outcomes comparable

Define the space, location and condition each option would provide. A new kitchen cannot solve a school catchment or an inaccessible staircase. Those benefits sit outside a cost calculator.

Compare total resources

Moving consumes transaction costs and may require more borrowing. Improving consumes a works budget and may add some property value. Our extension tool carries existing equity into both options and keeps spare cash in savings.

Test the assumptions that matter

Try higher building costs, a lower sale price and a smaller value uplift. The model assumes immediate work and identical borrowing terms, so adjust your expectations if finance is expensive or disruption is long. Renovation-specific staging is deferred from V1.
