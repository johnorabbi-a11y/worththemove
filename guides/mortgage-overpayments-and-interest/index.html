How mortgage overpayments affect interest

Overpayments reduce principal sooner. Keeping the ordinary payment unchanged can shorten the mortgage.

Two lender treatments

A lender might shorten the term or reduce future payments after an overpayment. Our model keeps the ordinary payment and shortens the term. If your lender recalculates payments instead, its future interest path will differ.

Compare the same money

Keeping cash in savings can earn interest and preserve access to it. The tool gives both options the same cash budget and saves unused amounts. Enter an after-tax savings rate rather than comparing a gross savings rate with a mortgage rate.

Check the restrictions

Read the product’s overpayment conditions before entering a large extra amount. No penalty is automatically deducted in this tool. Paying down debt may be financially attractive while still leaving too little available cash for repairs or emergencies.
