How to compare mortgage deals properly

Keep the starting balance, time horizon and repayment basis consistent before comparing rates.

Collect comparable inputs

Use the same balance and remaining term for each repayment mortgage. Record product fees and whether they are financed, plus legal, valuation and exit costs. Make sure the rates apply to your LTV and circumstances.

Look beyond one monthly number

Review cash needed at the start, interest and fees over the deal period, and the balance at the end. Extending the term can reduce the payment while increasing interest. Different initial deal lengths need an explicit assumption for what happens after the shorter one expires.

Check the parts the model cannot decide

Portability, overpayment rules, eligibility and exit charges can outweigh a small estimated cost difference. Read the lender’s illustration and ask questions about the features you expect to use. Our figures are a comparison aid, not a product recommendation.
