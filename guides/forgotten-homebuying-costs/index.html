Costs people forget when buying a home

The deposit is only one part of the cash required. Build a budget for completion and for the first year.

Before the keys

Obtain quotes for legal work and searches, a condition survey, mortgage fees and any specialist reports. Enter property tax from the official calculator for the nation and your ownership circumstances. A zero tax input is not confirmation of a relief.

After completion

Allow for repairs, locks, essential furniture, insurance and overlapping rent. Leasehold properties may have service charges, ground rent and major works exposure. Recurring costs belong in the monthly ownership input; immediate work belongs in setup costs.

Compare cash with cost

A deposit becomes equity, while a survey fee is spent. Our report shows both cash required and net housing cost, so a larger deposit is not automatically described as a loss. Keep an emergency reserve outside the completion budget.
