Costs people forget when moving house

A price gap is only part of a moving budget. Selling, buying and the overlap between homes create separate bills.

Start on the sale side

Record the agent’s fee including VAT, legal costs, mortgage redemption charges and removals. A percentage agent fee needs converting using the likely sale price, not the original purchase price.

Then price the purchase

Add the correct property tax, conveyancing, searches, survey and mortgage fees. Include storage or temporary accommodation if the chain does not complete neatly. Our legal and setup allowances are editable budgets, not local quotes.

Track when cash is needed

Some costs arrive before completion while sale equity is still tied up. A transaction can look affordable in total and still create a short-term cash gap. The calculators aggregate upfront costs; keep a separate dated cash plan for an actual move.
