Understanding loan-to-value

Loan-to-value expresses the mortgage as a percentage of the property value used by the lender.

Work it out

Divide the mortgage balance by the property value and multiply by 100. A £180,000 mortgage on a £200,000 property is 90% LTV. A £20,000 deposit supplies the other 10%, before fees.

Why the denominator matters

At a £190,000 valuation, the same £180,000 loan is about 94.7% LTV. Improving a property does not guarantee a matching increase in valuation. A falling market can offset principal repayments.

Use it with costs

LTV helps locate products you might qualify for, but does not identify the cheapest overall deal. Compare rates, fees and term as well. Our deposit examples use purchase price as value and exclude lender-specific treatment of financed fees.
