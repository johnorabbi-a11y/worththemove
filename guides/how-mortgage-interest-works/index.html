How mortgage interest actually works

A repayment mortgage pays both interest and principal. The split changes even when the payment stays the same.

The balance does the work

Interest is charged on the amount still owed. Early in a repayment mortgage, the balance is high, so more of each payment goes towards interest. As the balance falls, more of that same payment reduces the debt. A £200,000 balance at 5% incurs about £833 of interest in the first month under a monthly approximation.

What the monthly formula assumes

Our engine divides the annual nominal rate by 12 and calculates a level payment over the chosen term. Many lenders calculate interest daily, so actual statements can differ with dates and rounding. A zero-interest loan is handled separately: principal divided by the number of payments.

Compare at the same date

Total payments alone are incomplete. Two loans can have similar cash payments but different balances after five years. Use interest plus fees to compare financing costs, and look separately at debt remaining. The outstanding balance is an obligation, not a charge already paid.
