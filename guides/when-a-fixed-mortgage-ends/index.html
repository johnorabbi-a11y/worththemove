What happens when a fixed mortgage ends?

The fixed rate has an end date; the mortgage balance usually has many more years to run.

Separate the deal from the term

A five-year fix on a 25-year mortgage fixes the initial rate, not the repayment date. At the end of the deal, the balance is still owed. Check the offer for the rate that applies if you take no action.

Model the next payment

Use the remaining balance and remaining term with the possible next rate. The two-year versus five-year tool recalculates Option A at month 25 and includes a new fee. Try several future rates rather than treating one as certain.

Prepare for practical constraints

A new deal can involve valuation and affordability checks, fees and completion timing. Changes in income or property value may affect the options available. Confirm the expiry date and replacement terms directly with the lender.
