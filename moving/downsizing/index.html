What would change the result?

Change the rates, costs and period to test how robust this result is.

Existing equity is carried into the comparison. Buying and selling happen immediately. The same borrowing rate and term apply to both options; confirm actual finance and any permission requirements.

Released equity is sale value less the existing mortgage and replacement home price. Transaction costs are shown separately. Its interest is included using your after-tax savings rate.

Unspent cash and monthly budget differences earn the entered savings rate. Net cost is starting equity plus equal cash contributions minus ending equity and savings. Future sale costs and non-financial benefits are excluded.

Released equity is not profit created by moving. Transaction costs reduce existing wealth, while future running-cost savings may rebuild it.

How this comparison works

Sale proceeds first clear the existing mortgage and fund the replacement property. Any excess becomes savings; if equity is insufficient, the model uses a new repayment mortgage.

What is included

Repayment mortgage interest, cash needed at the start, entered running and transaction costs, and estimated equity at the comparison date. Future home value follows your annual growth assumption, including negative growth if entered.

What is not included

Automatic property tax, lender eligibility, inflation adjustment, future selling costs, uncertain legal obligations or the non-financial value of a home. Values are nominal, not discounted to today’s money. This is not a valuation or lending decision.

A worked example

With £300,000 as the current home value, the starting monthly outgoings are £592 for stay in current home and £250 for downsize. Over 60 months, net housing cost is £28,649 and £16,054 respectively. Questions about this calculation

Does the lower figure mean I should choose that option?

No. It means a lower modelled cost under the inputs. Eligibility, cash reserves, flexibility and the home you need are outside the calculation.

Is property purchase tax calculated automatically?

No. Enter the amount from the official calculator for England or Northern Ireland, Scotland, or Wales and verify reliefs and surcharges. The default of zero excludes tax from the illustration.
