Financial information disclaimer

The limits of these tools and the meaning of a cheaper modelled result.

Information, not advice

The website does not take account of your full financial position, needs, risk tolerance or eligibility. A lower modelled cost is not a recommendation and does not mean the option is suitable or affordable.

Estimates depend on assumptions

Rates, fees, tax, property prices, repair costs and rent can change. Future values are scenarios, not predictions. Monthly repayment approximations can differ from a lender’s daily interest calculation and payment dates. The tools do not establish the amount a lender will offer.

Before making a commitment

Check the lender’s formal illustration and redemption statement, verify the correct property tax, obtain relevant legal and survey advice, and consider cash reserves and practical needs. A mortgage is secured borrowing and failure to keep up payments can put the home at risk.
