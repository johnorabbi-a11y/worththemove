About Worth The Move

A transparent way to compare the financial consequences of home decisions.

Two choices, made comparable

Worth The Move helps people in the UK compare the costs of mortgages, buying and moving. It puts monthly payments beside upfront cash, interest and the debt or equity left at a common date.

The site is a calculation and education project. It does not arrange mortgages, sell products, assess eligibility or provide personalised financial advice. There are no affiliate links, paid placements or fabricated endorsements in this launch.

Open about the limits

Default rates and costs are illustrations. We do not claim access to live lender offers, a professional valuation or a regulated advice service. No qualifications, accreditations or named expert reviews are claimed.

Our methodology explains the arithmetic. The public project repository makes the code and changes available to inspect. Questions or corrections can be raised through the contact page.
