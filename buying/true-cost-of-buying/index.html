What would change the result?

Change the rates, costs and period to test how robust this result is.

The option needing less upfront cash retains and earns interest on the difference. Monthly payment differences are not reinvested. Legal, survey and removal allowances are shared. Enter actual mortgage quotes for both options.

Property tax is your entered amount, not an automatically calculated liability. Add service charges, insurance and maintenance to monthly ownership costs. Property growth is an editable scenario and can be negative.

A lender valuation is not necessarily a condition survey. Include leasehold charges and immediate repairs in your ownership budget.

How this comparison works

The completion budget includes the deposit, purchase tax, legal costs, survey and removals. The period comparison separates cash paid from equity accumulated.

What is included

Repayment mortgage interest, cash needed at the start, entered running and transaction costs, and estimated equity at the comparison date. Future home value follows your annual growth assumption, including negative growth if entered.

What is not included

Automatic property tax, lender eligibility, inflation adjustment, future selling costs, uncertain legal obligations or the non-financial value of a home. Values are nominal, not discounted to today’s money. This is not a valuation or lending decision.

A worked example

With £250,000 as the first home price, the starting monthly outgoings are £1,515 for home a and £1,584 for home b. Over 60 months, net housing cost is £62,550 and £68,799 respectively. These are the page’s default figures, not market quotes; changing the inputs above replaces the report.

A common mistake to avoid

A lender valuation is not necessarily a condition survey. Include leasehold charges and immediate repairs in your ownership budget.

Questions about this calculation

Does the lower figure mean I should choose that option?

No. It means a lower modelled cost under the inputs. Eligibility, cash reserves, flexibility and the home you need are outside the calculation.

Is property purchase tax calculated automatically?

No. Enter the amount from the official calculator for England or Northern Ireland, Scotland, or Wales and verify reliefs and surcharges. The default of zero excludes tax from the illustration.
