How to interpret it

A two-year deal gives a fixed fee little time to repay itself. Compare a smaller balance too: the fee stays £999 while the interest saving shrinks.

A fixed fee takes a larger share of a small mortgage. The calculated balance threshold shows where the fee-paying deal overtakes the no-fee deal over your chosen period.
