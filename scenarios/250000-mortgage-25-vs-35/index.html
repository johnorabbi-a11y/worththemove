How to interpret it

A ten-year extension has a visible monthly benefit and a long interest tail. Compare the full-term figure with the shorter five-year debt snapshot rather than treating either alone as the answer.

Ten extra years can substantially change the repayment path. Compare outstanding debt at the same date, not just payments across different end dates.
