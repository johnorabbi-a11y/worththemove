How to interpret it

The comparison gives the saver the same £200 each month. Mortgage interest avoided and savings interest earned are both counted, while access to the cash remains a separate consideration.

We maintain the normal mortgage payment while overpayments shorten the term. The alternative saves the same extra money; both save payments freed when the mortgage ends.
