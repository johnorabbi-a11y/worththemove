Contact and corrections

Report a calculation issue or suggest a clearer explanation.

Report an issue

Use the Worth The Move issue tracker to report an error or suggest an improvement. A GitHub account is needed to post. This is the project’s current contact route; there is no advice or customer-account service.

Help us reproduce the result

Include the calculator name, example numbers, expected result and what you saw. Round or substitute the figures if they relate to your own finances. Issues are public: do not post addresses, account numbers, mortgage documents or other personal information.

Questions about a financial decision

We cannot assess your circumstances or recommend a mortgage. Check product questions with the lender and tax or legal questions with an appropriate professional. MoneyHelper’s homebuying information is a further source of general guidance.
